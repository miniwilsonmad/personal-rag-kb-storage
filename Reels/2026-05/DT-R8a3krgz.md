Kyoto in one day 🌿
Start your morning at Kyoto Station,
enjoy the Hozugawa River Boat Ride,
grab a light lunch on the boat,
then explore Arashiyama in the afternoon 🎋

Nature, adventure, and sightseeing — all in one perfect day.

Save this for your Kyoto trip ✨

The Hozugawa River Boat Ride operates as usual, even on rainy days.
You can board from JR Kameoka Station and enjoy a peaceful journey through nature. 

Advance reservations are available, but walk-ins are also welcome.
Feel free to visit anytime.

Access
・From Torokko Kameoka Station:
Taxi (5 min) / Bus (10 min) / Horse-drawn carriage (30 min)
・From JR Kameoka Station: 8 min walk
